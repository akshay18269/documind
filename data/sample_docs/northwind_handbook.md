# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (fictional sample document)

## 1. Working Hours and Remote Work

Northwind Labs follows a hybrid work model. Employees are expected to be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and may work remotely on the other three days. Core collaboration hours are 10:30 AM to 4:00 PM local time, during which employees must be reachable on Slack. Employees may work fully remotely from another city for up to 30 days per calendar year with written manager approval. Working from another country requires approval from both the manager and the People Operations team and is limited to 14 days per year because of tax and legal rules.

## 2. Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Up to 8 unused days can be carried over to the next year; any additional unused days expire on March 31. Sick leave is 12 days per year and does not require a medical certificate for absences of up to 2 consecutive days. Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave, and paternity leave is 4 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time within the first 12 months after the child's birth or adoption. Bereavement leave is 5 days for immediate family members.

## 3. Learning and Development Budget

Every employee gets an annual learning budget of 60,000 rupees to spend on courses, books, certifications and conferences. Expenses must be pre-approved by the manager and claimed within 30 days of purchase. Employees who leave the company within 12 months of receiving reimbursement for a certification must repay that amount. Northwind Labs also runs an internal "Friday Tech Talk" every second Friday at 4:00 PM, where any employee can present.

## 4. Expense Reimbursement

Business travel must be booked through the company portal at least 7 days in advance. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Daily meal allowance during travel is 1,500 rupees in metro cities and 1,000 rupees elsewhere. Receipts are mandatory for any single expense above 500 rupees. Expense reports must be submitted within 15 days of returning from a trip, and reimbursements are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle.

## 5. Information Security

All company laptops use full-disk encryption and must be locked when unattended. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the code repository and the cloud console. Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage. Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team at security@northwind.example within 2 hours of discovery.

## 6. Performance Reviews and Promotions

Performance is reviewed twice a year, in April and in October. Ratings use a five-point scale from 1 (needs improvement) to 5 (outstanding). Promotion decisions are made only in the October cycle and require a rating of at least 4 in each of the two most recent reviews. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible for promotion or for internal transfers until the plan is closed.

## 7. Notice Period and Exit

The standard notice period is 60 days for all employees who have completed their probation of 6 months. During probation the notice period is 15 days. Employees must return all company equipment on their last working day, and the final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
